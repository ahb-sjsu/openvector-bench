# RC-10: the echo campaign — the density mechanism's physics fully mapped, its operating point one cell out of reach

**Registered close after the 4-sweep envelope** (64 arms, `R90`–`R93`,
drivers `harness/rc1/fam5a-d.py`, raw `results/r90-93.txt`), against
`RC10_PLAN.md`. P1/P2 not achieved in-envelope; the mechanism's physics
is now complete, with each sweep eliminating one confound:

1. **Off-manifold prototypes inflate dimension** (`R90`): random-direction
   protos push members off the corpus manifold — g1 18 → 26 with dose,
   g1exp *flattens*. But the same arms independently reproduced **real's
   neighbourhood anatomy for the first time** (same-article fraction
   0.70 vs real's 0.66, trio intact) and honest scatter to np95 33.
2. **On-manifold anchored groups reach real's density response**
   (`R91`): proto = a corpus row gives g1exp −0.153…−0.191 — real's
   range, by any mechanism, for the first time — and gspan −0.35…−0.38,
   past the eight-block edge. Cost: the anchor row is the near-parallel
   partner of every member and becomes a mega-hub (g6 2.9 → 8.0).
3. **Anchorless midpoints kill the hub AND the response** (`R92`):
   g6 back to 1.79–1.87, g1exp back to −0.08…−0.11.
4. **Tightness is not the variable** (`R93`): anchorless at α up to
   0.99 stays flat. The diagnosis is arithmetic, found post-hoc: the
   ladder at `echo_m0 = 48` put ~85% of echo mass in ~900-member groups
   — resolved at every density (38 members present even at 4% sampling),
   hence n-independent, hence flattening. The anchored form evaded this
   because every member–anchor pair resolves at P ≈ s regardless of
   group size.

**The successor cell, precisely specified and untried: small anchorless
groups** — k = 2–8 members (universe ~30k groups), tight α, on-manifold
midpoint protos. Every pair then resolves at P ≈ s (the anchored form's
response) with in-degree bounded by k−1 (the anchorless form's sane
hubness). One sweep decides it; the envelope here was spent finding it.
