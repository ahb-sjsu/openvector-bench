# RC-11: the density-response mechanism exists — small window-local echo groups

**Registered close after the 2-sweep envelope** (32 arms, `R94`/`R95`,
drivers `harness/rc1/fam6.py`, `fam6b.py`, raw `results/r94-95.txt`).
Both registered predictions CONFIRMED — the first campaign since RC-3 to
confirm its headline predictions rather than refute them.

## P1 confirmed: hub-free density response at real's depth

Small anchorless echo groups (k members around a midpoint prototype,
universe sized to k) deliver the g1exp response with bounded in-degree:
−0.170 (k4) → −0.228 (k2) at rate 0.15, deepening monotonically with
rate and inversely with k — precisely the every-pair-resolves-at-P≈s
physics `RC10_VERDICT` specified. g6 stays 1.81–1.90 throughout: the
anchor-hub problem is solved by construction.

## P2 confirmed: the window lever transfers, and over-delivers

Corpus-wide partners shallow gspan (the R85 physics); **window-local
groups (echo_win 100k) restore it and rescue g1 at the same time**:

| arm (p, k, window) | g1 | g1exp | gspan | np95 | misses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O7 (0.12, 3, 100k)** | **17.17 IN** | **−0.182 IN** | **−0.380 IN** | 14 | g5 −0.003, g8 −0.002, g4 |
| O6 (0.12, 2, 100k) | 16.01 IN | −0.198 IN | −0.371 IN | 15 | g5 −0.003, g8 −0.008, g4 |
| O15 (0.15, 2, 100k) | 15.83 IN | −0.221 IN | −0.366 IN | 19 | g5 −0.010, g8 −0.013, g4 |

**O7 co-holds g1 + g1exp + gspan + trend + rspan + g3 + g6 — a
combination never before achieved in any family** — with seed checks in
band (s137: −0.182/−0.206). Both structural density residuals sit inside
the eight-block bands that excluded every prior configuration. And the
same groups move partition scatter as a side effect (np95 8–19 at these
doses, 39–74 at higher rates in `R94`, all at honest occupancy 0.29–0.32)
— confirming RC-8's conjecture that the density response and scatter are
one structure.

## What remains, and how precisely

Two knife-edge dose costs, both with mapped levers untried in
combination: **g5** (−0.003…−0.010; contrast compression from resolved
pairs — fine-scale rebalance) and **g8** (−0.002…−0.013; `pool_alpha`
was the g8 lever, `R70`). Plus the eternal g4. An RC-12 composition
round holds the echo cell fixed and sweeps only those two levers; if
they close without disturbing the co-held seven, the freeze candidate
plausibly stands at **9/10 robust with real density response and
meaningful scatter** — and earns the ≥8-block one-shot.
