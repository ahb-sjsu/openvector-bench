the expected outcome, all declared before evaluation). Records:
`results/rc2_heldout.json` (real side), `results/rc2_generator.json`

## 1. Held-out real targets

Four 600k-row blocks of the Cohere Embed-V3 Wikipedia corpus at offsets
never touched by any prior round: 5,000,000 / 15,000,000 / 25,000,000 /
39,000,000. Bands are mean ± 2 sd (ddof 1) across blocks — the same
derivation as the registered bands, on fresh data. This services
`PROFILE.md` falsifier P1.

Two facts the held-out side established about the *registered* targets
before the generator entered:

* **Real's g6 at fresh offsets is 1.72–1.74, not 1.696** — the registered
  target carried block-0 fortune. The held-out band is [1.711, 1.748].
* **The held-out §3 trend band sits higher than the registered one** —
  [0.455, 0.658] vs [0.254, 0.649]: fresh blocks are steeper than the
  blocks the band was registered on. g4's held-out band is strikingly
  tight: [352, 364] (block sd 2.9).

## 4. Adjudication

**EXCLUDED.** The mandatory trio fails on g6 (1.773 vs [1.711, 1.748]);
5 of 24 held-out comparisons are in band. This is the outcome the freeze
predicted (`RC2_FREEZE.md` §3), now held-out and registered instead of
in-sample. The specific content of the verdict:

1. **What the family got right, held out**: intrinsic dimension (g1, in a
   held-out band for the first time in the campaign), distance contrast
   (g5), and near-misses of 1–3% on hubness (g6), PCA retention (g8), and
   effective rank (g3). The fixed-density neighbourhood geometry is
   essentially matched.
2. **What excludes it**: the density response. The §3 trend (0.377) sits in
   the *registered* band [0.254, 0.649] — cycle b's mechanism did what it
   claimed — but the held-out blocks are steeper (band [0.455, 0.658]), and
   the generator misses them. The §3b spans and mid-pool ratios fail by
   large factors, as `R66` predicted from their 4× seed-spread-to-band
   ratio. g4 remains 23% high.
3. **The band-shift finding**: real's own g6 (1.73 vs registered 1.696) and
   §3 trend ([0.455, 0.658] vs [0.254, 0.649]) moved between the registered
   blocks and the held-out blocks — block-to-block drift in the real corpus
   is comparable to several of the admission windows. Any future RC round
   should derive bands from more than four blocks before registering them.

RC-1 therefore stands **unadmitted**: no generator in this family passes
the registered slate, and the campaign's structured search says the misses
are family-level. The deliverable of the arc is the frozen, bit-exact,
random-access generator (the best-characterized synthetic embedding
geometry we know of, with its misses quantified against held-out data) plus
the measurement infrastructure and the registered negative.
