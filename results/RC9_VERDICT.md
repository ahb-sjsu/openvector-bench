# RC-9: both density kills fire in one sweep — and the cascade is a scatter mechanism instead

**Registered close after Phase A.** 16 arms (`R89`, driver
`harness/rc1/fam4a.py`, raw `results/r89.txt`), against `RC9_PLAN.md`'s
pre-registered kills. The plan said P1 ∧ P2 killing stops the campaign;
both killed in the first sweep.

## P1/P2: the cascade flattens the density response at every scale

Replacing the arrangement with a deep geometric cascade (cluster sizes
2^L articles, L = 1…14, keyed-random, per-level frames) moves g1exp the
WRONG way at every decay setting — −0.004…−0.087 against base −0.113
and real's −0.16…−0.21 — and gspan with it (−0.02…−0.27 vs real's
−0.35…−0.58). The resolution argument was right about *when* structure
resolves and wrong about *what happens then*: a resolved shared-subspace
component (a `casc_dim`-dimensional frame draw) pulls members together
along a moderate-dimensional direction set, which does not lower the
TwoNN reading — while its unresolved counterpart adds apparent variance
at low density. Net: flattening.

**The refined family-level statement, now supported at every scale
tried**: additive shared-component structure — chapters (m ≈ 400
articles, `R75`), the cascade (m = 2…16,384, this round), topics
(`R81`–`R84`), the continuum sheet (`R86`–`R88`) — flattens the density
response regardless of scale, sharing count, or amplitude. The ONLY
mechanism that steepens it is **near-parallel resolution structure**
(the dup ladder, α → 1: resolved pairs are ~zero-dimensional), and its
single-dose form fails held-out (`RC8_VERDICT`). If real's density
response is reproducible in this framework at all, it requires
near-parallel structure across a *spectrum* of scales — article-level
and group-level near-duplication with graded blend strengths — which is
a new mechanism class, recorded for a successor, untried here.

## P3: the best honest partition scatter ever measured

At decay 0.9 the cascade reaches **np95 = 34 with the mandatory trio
intact and occupancy CV 0.33** (real: 47–50 and 0.39) — no outlier
components, same-article fraction 0.95. Two independent arms confirm
(G3, G12). The cost at that setting: g3 272 (spectrum blown), g8 0.72,
trend ~0, spans dead. The cascade is therefore a genuine *scatter*
mechanism — the strongest known — whose composition with a
geometry-preserving configuration is the scatter problem's best lead:
its cross-cell neighbours come from mid-scale cluster bonds that
k-means cannot tile, exactly the structure R80 found missing.
