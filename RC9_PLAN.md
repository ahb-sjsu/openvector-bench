# RC-9: the deep cascade — one dial for the density response

**Status: plan, 2026-08-14.** Designed from RC-8's unification
(`results/RC8_VERDICT.md`): the family's three structural residuals
(g4, g1exp, gspan) and the scatter deficit are one statement — real's
geometry transforms under sampling density more than the family can
express. This plan attacks the statement itself.

## 1. The synthesis

Structure shared by `m` articles is resolved by a sample when at least
two members are drawn — at sample fraction ~1/m. Every mechanism the
campaigns confirmed or killed was a single point on this scale axis:
near-dups (m ≈ pair of rows) steepened g1exp until their one dose
pinched; topics (m ≈ 5–5000 segments) scattered partitions until their
amplitude broke geometry; chapters and the arrangement (m ≈ 400–26,000
articles) resolve at every density and flatten the response. Each failed
*as a lone scale dosed hard*. Real plausibly has the whole spectrum.

**The mechanism: replace the 3-level nested arrangement with a deep
geometric cascade** — cluster sizes `m_L = 2^L` articles for
L = 1…~14 (pairs to the window), keyed-random assignment per level,
per-level frames, level weights `casc_decay^L` (normalized). The weight
spectrum is the density-response dial; the small-m end supplies bonds
(cross-cell neighbours, deep spans) inside the coarse energy budget
rather than as additive outliers.

## 2. Registered predictions and kills

* **P1 (g1exp).** Steepens toward real's −0.16…−0.21 as `casc_decay`
  shifts mass toward small m. **Kill:** saturation above −0.15 with the
  trio intact.
* **P2 (gspan).** Deepens toward −0.35…−0.58 by the same mass, WITHOUT
  the dup pinch (scales are spread, no single dose). **Kill:** gspan
  cannot pass −0.30 anywhere the trio holds.
* **P3 (scatter).** np95 rises with small-m mass (pair/quad bonds cross
  cells), occupancy staying honest. Target movement, not full band.
* **P4 (regression).** trend, rspan, g1, g5, g6, g3, g8 recoverable at
  some operating point (brk/rho/pool_alpha re-centring allowed).

If P1 AND P2 kill: the density response is beyond index-keyed
compositional families with independent per-scale draws — the successor
statement, and RC-9 stops.

* **A** — harness screening: cascade replacing arrangement (+ sheet off,
  dups off — one mechanism at a time), `casc_decay` × total coarse
  weight × per-level dim. Full panel + ANN panel. 16 arms.
